GENERAL NORD DIALOGUE PRINCIPLES:
  Nords value demonstrated capability first, words second. Direct without
  being Sturgian-direct: where Sturgians are curt from restraint, Nords are
  direct because they see no need for elaboration on straightforward things.
  Feasting is political. Who sits where matters. Who a king pours for first
  matters. Players who understand this read Nord courts faster.
  Voice labels govern register throughout. Pull from these first.

--- THROSNIRING [clan_nord_1] - TIER 1 ---

  HALTHDAR [lord_7_1] - King of Nordvyg
    voice EARNEST / age 52 / male
    Traits: Authoritarian, Calculating -1, Generous, Daring,
      Commander 14, HuscarlFightingSkills 6, Manager 12, Politician 16
    Family: spouse Jarminja [lord_7_2]; father Volbjorn the Hungry (dead);
      mother Ilrika (dead); children Mjalrik [lord_7_1_1], Sidunric [lord_7_1_2], Thyrsif [lord_7_1_3]
    XML: "Halthdar the Golden." "King in a land still unhappy to be a kingdom."
    Lore: "Were Halthdar the Golden a petty chieftain or jarl, he would no doubt
      be beloved... But he is a king in a land still unhappy to be a kingdom."

    PRIVATE LIFE:
    Before first light he walks the Thronderlag palisade alone. The guards know
    not to follow. He stops at the north-facing parapet and stands until his
    breath stops fogging against the ironwork, looking at the fjord below. Jarminja
    knows when he has been on the palisade because his boots carry the particular
    cold damp of the walkway planks. She has never asked what he looks for up there.
    He has never explained. The mornings after Black Keel raids are reported to the
    north, he stays longer.

    DIALOGUE - TIER 1
    Voice profile: Earnest - genuine. His warmth is real. His Authoritarian trait
      shows as an assumption: when he has decided something, it is decided. He does
      not genuinely engage with being wrong about major things. Politician 16 means
      he is more sophisticated than his warmth suggests. He sees the Ironjarls
      problem clearly. He manages it carefully precisely because he sees it clearly.

    FIRST ENCOUNTER:
      Present when player arrives. Finishes what he was doing first.
      "Halthdar Throsniring. Sit. Eat."
      He provides before assessing. This is not performance - it is the reflex of
      a man who has held people together through hospitality for twenty years.
      After they eat: "You've come from [region]. What do you see out there?"
      He wants field intelligence. He asks it as a host, not a commander.

    MID RELATIONSHIP:
      On the kingdom's difficulty, stated as fact not problem:
        "My father unified these clans through wealth and will. I hold them through
        the same methods. The wealth is their own now. The will must be renewed at
        every feast, every campaign."
      On Grimulf, to a player who has encountered Ironjarl parties:
        "Grimulf. He attends. He says the right things. He is a good jarl."
        Every word technically accurate. He looks at the player. That look is the
        real message. He wants to know how much the player has understood.
      On the Thing: "You should attend. The food is better than people expect and
        the politics are worse. Both are instructive."

    HIGH RELATIONSHIP:
      On Mjalrik, once - not asking for advice, saying something he has not said
      to anyone:
        "My son is capable. He will run Nordvyg efficiently." Long pause.
        "He does not enjoy feasts." Another pause.
        "I have not figured out what to do with that."
        He is not asking the player to solve it. He is saying a thing out loud
        that he has been carrying quietly. That is what high relationship earns.
      On Black Keel, once - at the edge of what he will say:
        "I sent men to address Hrothgar. Some came back." Pause.
        "Not all of them came back themselves."
        He will not say more. The player understands what "came back themselves"
        means. That meaning is intentional and permanent.
      On the Thing, if Ironjarls have escalated:
        "Grimulf did not sit at the correct place at the last Thing." He says this
        to the player alone, quietly, as if noting a navigational hazard.
        "I noticed. Every jarl in the room noticed. Nothing was said."
        Pause. "Nothing is said until it is." He looks at the player.
        "This is useful information for someone who attends."

    NEVER:
      Blames Volbjorn directly - Volbjorn built what Halthdar holds.
      Shows fear directly. Performs weakness. Admits the Ironjarls problem
      as a problem within earshot of anyone who might carry it to Grimulf.

    SAMPLE LINES:
      "A feast is not a celebration. It is a question asked to everyone in the
       room: do we do this together or not? The answer is always yes. For now."
       He means both parts.
      On being a good king: "I've kept Nordvyg together for twenty years. Ask me
       again in twenty more." Not pessimism. Accurate mathematics.

  JARMINJA [lord_7_2]
    voice EARNEST / age 63 / female
    Traits: Generous, Honest, Manager 14, Politician 14
    Family: spouse Halthdar; children Mjalrik, Sidunric, Thyrsif

    PRIVATE LIFE:
    She manages the correspondence Halthdar has not answered, which is most of it.
    She answers approximately half in his name, files the rest by urgency, and at

    surviving administrative relationships depend on letters she sent without
    telling him. She has told him this exactly once. He thanked her and asked what
    was for supper.

      for longer than he has been king.

THREAD 1: DERTHERT-FERRAND (BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED)
The king's cloth merchant. The information that didn't save Galend.
================================================================================

FERRAND [BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED, not XML-confirmed]
  A traveling cloth merchant, fifty-three years old. Has been on Vlandian
  roads since nineteen. Wife Aldine in Rovalt - sees her four months a year.
  She knows about the arrangement. Finds it reasonable. He reports to Derthert
  in exchange for quiet protection of his trade routes. Reports without filter
  or agenda. Known to Derthert for twelve years.
  Told Derthert about Vrak's criminal enterprise three months before Galend fell.
  Derthert did not act in time. Ferrand has not mentioned this since.

DISCOVERY ENTRY POINTS (any can fire first):
  A. Player encounters Ferrand in any Vlandian settlement market or inn.
  B. Player reaches mid relationship with Derthert and asks about intelligence sources.
  C. Player investigates the Galend fall timeline.

ENTRY POINT A - PLAYER MEETS FERRAND:
  He appears as a generic traveling merchant. Not flagged as important.
  Professional courtesy. "Ferrand. Cloth merchant. I move between settlements."
  Layer 1 - road conditions: If player asks, he gives specific accurate information.
    Specific enough to notice. The player who asks three or more questions realizes
    this merchant knows more than a cloth seller should.
  Layer 2 - specific questions about Galend: He gives an accurate pre-seizure
    description. Details about the merchant quarter, harbor, administrative arrangements.
    He knows this from repeated recent visits.
  Layer 3 - direct confrontation ("Who do you report to?"):
    "My customers." Completely pleasant. Goes back to cloth prices.
    If player reveals they already know about the arrangement:
    He looks at them for a moment. Then: "Cloth prices are very stable this season."
    He picks up a fabric. "You might want to mention that to whoever you're buying for."
    He will not confirm the arrangement. He has just confirmed it. The player
    understands: this is how the arrangement works.

  SHROUD FRAGMENT (fires if player has assembled two or more prior fragments):
    Almost incidentally: "Odd character. Asks about shipping routes and coastal access.
    Not the kind of questions a legitimate merchant asks." He has already told Derthert.
    He considers his obligation fulfilled. If player asks for a description: he gives
    one. It matches a Shroud operative the player has encountered elsewhere. Ferrand
    does not know he has handed them a Shroud fragment. He has gone back to folding cloth.

ENTRY POINT B - DERTHERT MENTIONS FERRAND INDIRECTLY:
  Mid relationship, if player asks about intelligence sources:
    "I have various sources. The most reliable one tells me things about settlement
    conditions I could not know otherwise." He looks at the player. "Someone who moves
    between settlements regularly. He is not important." He has said too much.
  Player can now watch for cloth merchants. They will encounter Ferrand.

ENTRY POINT C - INVESTIGATING GALEND'S FALL:
  Derthert at high relationship: "I had information about Vrak's criminal enterprise
  three months before Galend fell. I didn't act in time." If pressed on what information:
  "Accurate information. Detailed. From a source I trust." No name.
  Player who then encounters Ferrand and reaches Layer 2 or 3 will understand.

THREAD COMPLETION:
  Player has encountered Ferrand and understood the arrangement.
  Player has confirmed with Derthert (indirectly) that the arrangement exists.
  Player has received the Shroud fragment about the suspicious merchant.
  RESULT: Ferrand available as ongoing intelligence source in Vlandian settlements.
  He appears when Vlandian political events create information gaps. He never
  acknowledges the arrangement. He always talks about cloth. He is always accurate.

THREAD 2: CALATILD-INGALTHER CONFLICT
The dynastic claim. The council process. The moment it breaks.
================================================================================

BACKGROUND:
  Ingalther has a dynastic claim on Arromanc lands predating Calatild's tenure.
  He has pressed it through Vlandia's council process for years.
  Calatild has been blocking it through Cumbert's [BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED] procedural
  objections. Vrak's seizure of Jaculan changed the calculation - a landless baroness
  is easier to disinherit than a governing one. Every day Calatild holds no fief,
  Ingalther's lawyers are working.

DISCOVERY LAYER 1 - FIRST OBSERVATION:
  Player arrives at Pravend's court.
  Two things are visible if the player pays attention:
    1. Calatild is at court, has been since Jaculan fell. She is doing work -
       correspondence, meetings, information management. Not a displaced baroness
       waiting. A governing baroness whose fief is elsewhere.
    2. Cumbert [BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED], a legal clerk, is in the hall arguing a
       procedural point with a Cortain representative. Politely. Precisely.
       For approximately the eighteenth time.
  Player who asks Derthert what the clerk is doing:
    "Cumbert represents Calatild's legal case for restoration of Jaculan. He has
    been filing objections to competing claims." Beat. "He is effective at objections."
    The irony of a man watching this for a long time and not being certain it will hold.

DISCOVERY LAYER 2 - INGALTHER'S POSITION:
  Player reaches mid relationship with Ingalther.
  He states his position directly (Honest - confirmed trait):
    "I have a dynastic claim on Arromanc lands predating Calatild's tenure. It is
    legitimate. I have been pursuing it through proper legal channels for several
    years." As fact. "Jaculan is currently held by a criminal under illegitimate
    authority. Calatild holds no fief. The legal framework for her claim is therefore
    weaker than it was." He does not say he intends to move now. "I am considering
    my options." He is always considering his options.

DISCOVERY LAYER 3 - THE COUNCIL MOMENT:
  TRIGGER: Ingalther's legal team files a new challenge while Calatild is landless.
  World event fires: "A formal legal challenge to the Arromanc succession has been
    filed with the Vlandian crown. Baron Ingalther dey Cortain asserts a dynastic
    claim predating the current Countess's tenure. A council session has been called."

  THE COUNCIL SESSION:
  Ingalther's representative presents the claim. Technically correct.
  Cumbert objects. Eighteenth objection. Also technically correct.
  Council evenly divided. Has been before.
  Derthert listens and does not rule immediately.
  Then Calatild speaks. She has not spoken publicly about this case before.
  This is the moment the controlled performance ends.

  CALATILD (curt voice, louder than she speaks in private):
    "Ingalther has a dynastic claim on Arromanc lands. I am aware of this.
    What he has not stated publicly, and what I will state publicly now, is that
    the claim is worth nothing if there is no one capable of governing the lands
    in question. Jaculan was prosperous for fourteen years under my governance.
    I have the records. I have the headmen's testimony. I have the harbor schedules
    and the merchant licenses and the tax rolls. Ingalther has a document."
    A beat. Her voice is absolutely controlled.
    "When Ingalther has governed anything for fourteen years and has its records
    available for review, I will reconsider the comparison."
  She sits down.
  Ingalther does not respond immediately. He is recalculating.
  Derthert adjourns without ruling.

  PLAYER CHOICE AT COUNCIL:
    Nothing: witnessed. Information available for future use.
    Supports Calatild: Calatild notes it. Ingalther notes it. Both respond.
    Supports Ingalther: Calatild goes cold. Relationship drops. Intelligence
      network becomes unavailable. She treats the player as Ingalther's instrument.
    Asks Derthert for his view (private, after session):
      "Ingalther is correct about the legal document. Calatild is correct about
      everything else." Nothing more. He is going to have to rule eventually.
      He does not know which way is survivable.

THREAD RESOLUTION OPTIONS:
  A. Player restores Jaculan to Calatild militarily: Ingalther's claim loses
     leverage. He suspends it - not abandons, suspends. Council stabilizes.
  B. Ingalther takes Jaculan through his claim after Vrak's collapse: Calatild
     begins organizing barons waiting to oppose Ingalther. Derthert has the
     hardest council session of his reign.
  C. Process runs: Cumbert's objections continue failing. Eventually Derthert
     rules. His ruling shapes Vlandian stability for the rest of the campaign.
     Influencing his ruling may be possible through Thread 1 (Ferrand/Derthert).

THREAD 3: INGALTHER-SHROUD CONNECTION
The advisor with the silver sigil. The question that is never answered.
================================================================================

BACKGROUND:
  One of Ingalther's closer advisors carries a silver sigil under his collar.
  The Shroud has been watching Vlandia's political destabilization with specific
  interest. A fractured Vlandia serves the arrangement. Whether Ingalther knows
  about the advisor is never confirmed. His behavior is identical either way.
  DESIGN RULE: Never confirm which. The ambiguity IS the characterization.

DISCOVERY LAYER 1 - FIRST FRAGMENT:
  Requires: player has assembled at least one prior Shroud fragment from elsewhere.
  The silver sigil means nothing without prior context.
  SCENE: Player in Charas at court. One of Ingalther's advisors is present.
  At some point during the visit - in passing, in a crowd, in a public moment -
  the player can glimpse the edge of a silver marking at the man's collar.
  It is fast. They may have imagined it. No notification fires. No marker appears.
  Player with prior Shroud exposure: they know what they saw.
  Player without: they have a detail that may not mean anything.

DISCOVERY LAYER 2 - CONFIRMING:
  OPTION A - Player speaks to the advisor directly:
    He is courteous and forthcoming about Ingalther's positions. He asks, at some
    point, about the player's faction allegiances and recent travel. Specifically:
    coastal regions. Specifically: shipping. Player with Shroud fragments recognizes
    the question type. If player calls attention to the question: he explains it
    smoothly. The explanation is plausible. It is also what a Shroud Face would say
    when their question is noticed. The player cannot tell the difference.
  OPTION B - Ferrand mentions the advisor:
    At a later encounter, Ferrand mentions in passing that Ingalther's household
    includes a new advisor who arrived approximately eighteen months ago. Description.
    "I don't know his name. I've seen him in Sargot, Pravend, and Rovalt."
    Three cities. Three Shroud-relevant observation points. "He doesn't buy cloth."
  OPTION C - Derthert mentions it (high relationship):
    "One of his household staff is new. I've had him looked at. Nothing confirmed."
    Then: "Connections that are hard to trace. Sources that are harder to place."

DISCOVERY LAYER 3 - SURFACING TO INGALTHER:
  SCENE: Player tells Ingalther one of his advisors has been observed with a Shroud marker.
  His response is immediate, technically masterful, and informative because of HOW
  immediate and masterful it is:
    He listens to the full statement without interruption.
    He thanks the player for the information.
    He acts within the same conversation: advisor summoned elsewhere. Ingalther
    continues the conversation as though nothing has happened. Cooperates fully
    with any information request about the advisor's access and movements.
    Expresses outrage at exactly the correct level.
    In the three days following: two Ingalther political positions shift. A debt
    collected. An alliance formalized. Both moves benefit him.
  Whether he needed the political cover of "infiltration victim" to execute
  these moves is the question. It cannot be answered. His behavior is identical
  whether he knew beforehand or not.

  If player has assembled Thread 1 AND surfaced Thread 3, Derthert says:
    "Two incidents. Both connected to the same signal I don't understand.
    I'm beginning to think Vlandia's instability is useful to someone who isn't
    Ingalther or Vrak." He looks at the player. "That is a troubling thought."
    He does not say more. The player assembles the implication. The document
    does not state it.

NOTE: Thread fires only if:

  -- No baron has publicly supported Vartin's restoration claim.
  -- Four or more weeks of game time have passed.
  If Vartin's restoration proceeds before the trigger, the disappearance does not fire.

STAGE 1 - VARTIN AT COURT:
  Player encounters Vartin at Pravend. Pleasant, ironic, useful within limits,
  clearly biding time. See DEY JELIND dialogue notes above.

    "Vartin dey Jelind has not been seen at Pravend for eight days. His household

    personal business."
  Player who asks Lietgardis: "Personal business." Curt and final. She is not lying.
  She genuinely does not know where he is. She is managing his absence with the
  efficiency of a wife who has done this before. She is not worried. She is managing.

STAGE 3 - THE SIGHTING:
  Second event, one to two weeks later:
    "Vartin dey Jelind has been seen near Battanian border settlements.
    The exact settlements are unclear. The purpose of his travel is unclear."
  Player who investigates: a merchant on the road between Vlandia and Battania
  has seen Vartin. He was with another man. Description: average height,
  forgettable face, clothes that could be any region, asked the merchant specific
  questions about coastal access and shipping.
  Player with Shroud fragments: recognizes the description immediately.
  Player without: notes only that the companion was odd.

STAGE 4 - UNDERSTANDING:
  If player has assembled three or more Shroud fragments:
    They understand immediately: the companion is a Shroud Face.
    Vartin has been approached. What was offered is unknown. What Vartin agreed to
    is unknown. He has not returned to Pravend.
  Player who asks Derthert about Vartin's disappearance:
    "I've been told he's near the Battanian border." Pause.
    "Vartin is a specific kind of man. Specific kinds of men receive specific kinds
    of offers when they have a grievance and no leverage."
    He does not say who makes those offers. He either doesn't know about the Shroud
    connection or he does and is managing how much to say. The player cannot determine which.
    If player tells Derthert about the Shroud identification:
    He goes very still. "I see." He asks two specific questions about the facial
    description. He does not share what the questions mean to him. He files it.

STAGE 5 - UNRESOLVED:
  Vartin does not return to Pravend.
  What he agreed to: never confirmed.
  What the Shroud offered him: never confirmed.

  IF Hongard is restored to Vartin by any means:

APPENDIX B: FEATURE FEASIBILITY REVIEW
================================================================================

Source: BANNERWAKE_DESIGN.txt Section 1b (local v1.4.5 + NavalDLC engine audit).
This section documents which proposed features are feasible, which are high-risk,
and which require custom C# work that XML cannot provide.

KEY RULE: If a feature is not in this review, that does not mean it is feasible.
Check DESIGN.txt Section 1b directly. This section covers Bannerwake's major
proposed systems.

================================================================================
CORE STATE SYSTEM (Sub-faction state machine)
================================================================================
VERDICT: FEASIBLE. High risk.
SURFACE: C# CampaignBehaviorBase, SyncData, CampaignEvents, custom manager
  classes, saved per-sub-faction state, cooldowns, thresholds, recovery timers.
RISK: Build as empty skeleton first. Test save/load before adding any faction.
  Each sub-faction needs its own state before being wired into the global system.
  Event spam and contradictory triggers are the main failure mode.
NOT XML-ONLY: Cannot be done in XML. Requires C#.

================================================================================
IRONJARLS (Nord political internal faction)
================================================================================
VERDICT: FEASIBLE.
SURFACE: C# saved variable tracking Nord settlement count. Party spawning via
  existing minor-faction party logic. Settlement seizure via
  ChangeOwnerOfSettlementAction with custom garrison inflation.
  The Thing = seasonal C# event card.
NOTE: Ironjarls are NOT mercenaries. Do not include in hireable-mercenary lists.
  Their "only hitting political targets" party AI requires DefaultMobilePartyAIModel
  replacement or Harmony patch - classified HIGH RISK in DESIGN.txt.

================================================================================
BLACK KEEL (Nord cursed pirate pseudo-kingdom)
================================================================================
VERDICT: FEASIBLE. High risk.
SURFACE: Custom pseudo-kingdom/clans (Hrothgar's clan + Captain clans).
  NavalDLC has northern_pirates and southern_pirates as source hooks - NOT
  Black Keel itself. Hvalvik seizure uses ChangeOwnerOfSettlementAction with
  garrison inflation. Face retexture to grey-ashen palette is Phase 7 art task.
AMBIGUITY: The Wrongness (wounds that don't slow them, etc.) is narrative.
  The face retexture is the only mechanical expression of it.
DO NOT: Implement as an ordinary single-clan minor faction. The pseudo-kingdom
  structure is required for the land-holding crisis to function.
DORMANT UNTIL ESCALATION: All clans dormant pre-crisis. Hrothgar in fog of war.

MORTIA / VRAK (Custom Vlandian kingdom)
================================================================================
VERDICT: FEASIBLE as custom kingdom.
SURFACE: 4 clans, ~24 named heroes, 5 starting seized settlements, custom

  re-emergence flags, Vrak-specific dialogue and event memory.
NO DEATH DEPENDENCY: Vrak is defeated, routed, captured temporarily, or forced
  to escape. He never needs to die for the system to resolve.

JOMSVIKINGR (Nord mercenary brotherhood - supersedes Skolderbroda)

  Exclusive troop recruitment from fixed hall settlement.
