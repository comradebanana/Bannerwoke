FACTION OVERVIEW:
  Eight clans aligned under the Gundaroving Grand Prince, which is not the same
  as a kingdom. Raganvad holds the title because his family has held it for three
  generations and because no other clan has been able to unseat them, not because
  anyone has agreed in principle that the Gundaroving should hold it permanently.
  The Boyar Council is the mechanism by which this distinction is continuously
  managed. Every policy Raganvad wants requires the Council's agreement, or its
  silence, or a version of it that can survive without explicit endorsement. He has
  been navigating this for fifteen years. He is very good at it. He has not yet
  made himself a king.

  The Sturgian character is direct without being warm, loyal without being
  sentimental, and violent when necessary without being cruel by preference
  (the exceptions are noted in individual lord entries). The Kuloving and
  Gundaroving are the senior military clans. The Vagiroving are the outward-facing
  trading and diplomatic arm. The Ormidoving are the raiders. The Vezhoving hold
  a strategic position with a despicable lord. The Kostoroving are quietly run by
  a seventy-three-year-old woman whose son does not know how much he depends on her.

  The wound in Sturgian politics that never fully healed: Olek's sister Varra
  died under unknown circumstances the year after Pendraic. Not at Pendraic, which
  would have been comprehensible. The year after, separately, at twenty years old,
  cause never confirmed. The Kuloving have never been given an explanation. Olek
  manages this the way he manages everything: without performing it.

FACTION TENSIONS:
  THE BOYAR COUNCIL: Raganvad wants to transform the Grand Prince title into
    genuine monarchy. The boyars want the arrangement they have — consultation,
    coalition, the permanent option to disagree. This is not a crisis. It is the
    permanent condition of Sturgian politics. Every feast, every campaign decision,
    every appointment is a data point in a decades-long argument about whether
    Sturgia is a kingdom or a persistent alliance. Raganvad has been moving the
    answer slowly toward kingdom. The Council has been moving it back.

  THE VARRA QUESTION: Olek's sister died the year after Pendraic. Cause unknown.
    The Kuloving have the specific grief of people who know something happened and
    have never been told what. Other clans treat this with careful neutrality — they
    do not raise the subject and do not prevent the Kuloving from raising it. Nobody
    raises it. The question sits in Sturgian politics as a permanent unresolved
    element, the kind that accumulates weight the longer it remains unresolved.

  DAKHILA'S CONTROL: The Kostoroving have been run by a seventy-three-year-old
    matriarch since before her son was old enough to be clan head. Rolan leads
    formally. Dakhila governs through him. Everyone senior enough to know the
    difference knows the difference. Forim (Rolan's son, 29) wants something
    different from the clan than either his father or his grandmother. He has not
    moved on this because Dakhila is alive and Rolan has not changed.

  VASHORKI'S POSITION: He is despicable by common consensus and holds Ov Castle,
    which is strategically significant. Raganvad needs the Vezhoving aligned.
    Vashorki prices this need. Raganvad pays it. Neither party pretends this is
    something other than what it is. Ratagost (the mystical, kind extended Vezhoving
    member who holds a castle as well) is as unlike Vashorki as one clan can contain.
    The Vezhoving are not one thing.

--- GUNDAROVING [clan_sturgia_1] ---
  CLAN OVERVIEW: The ruling clan. Three generations of Grand Princes have made
    the Gundaroving name synonymous with Sturgian central authority, which is
    a thing many Sturgians support in theory and resist in application. Raganvad
    is the best politician the line has produced. His wife Asta governs the
    relationships he manages insufficiently. His children are efficient and cold
    in ways that will matter when they lead. His uncle Vidar is loyal and adequate.
    The Gundaroving have what they need to hold Sturgia together. They have not
    yet built what they need to transform it.

  RAGANVAD [lord_2_1] — Grand Prince of Sturgia
    voice CURT / age 37 / male
    Traits: Authoritarian, Calculating, Commander 14, Closefisted, Devious,
      HuscarlFightingSkills 6, Manager 12, Cruel, Politician 16, Daring
    Family: spouse Asta [lord_2_2]; children Valla [lord_2_10], Simir [lord_2_7],
      Mimir [lord_2_7_1]
    XML: "Ragenvad, Hardrada type." "Genuine monarchist."

    LORE:
    He is Authoritarian not from vanity but from theory: Sturgia requires centralized
    rule to function under pressure, and the Boyar Council — for all its legitimacy —
    is too slow and too fractured to govern in a crisis. He has been working toward
    genuine monarchy for fifteen years through feasting, coalition-building, strategic
    generosity, and the kind of patience that requires calculation rather than nature.
    He is 37. He started young. Politician 16 is the number that matters: he reads
    rooms better than he lets on, remembers what people want and what they fear, and
    uses both with precision that the Curt voice conceals.

    He believes in Asta completely, which is his one unguarded position. He relies on
    her to manage the relationships his manner closes off and the correspondence his
    schedule does not reach. He knows she answers his letters in his name. He has
    thanked her for this once. He will not raise it again because there is nothing
    left to say about it. The arrangement works. The arrangement has been working for
    twenty years.

    PRIVATE LIFE:
    Before campaign meetings he sits with the map for twenty minutes alone. Not
    planning — he has already planned. He is memorizing the position of every lord
    who will be present and what each of them said the last time this configuration
    assembled. He does not take notes. He reviews. When the meeting begins he knows
    exactly what each person in the room wants and what they are prepared to trade
    for it. He has been doing this since he was twenty-three.

  ASTA [lord_2_2]
    voice EARNEST / age 37 / female / culture STURGIAN (confirmed, not Nordic)
    Traits: Generous, Honest, Manager 14, Politician 14
    Family: spouse Raganvad; children Valla, Simir, Mimir

    LORE:
    Sturgian culture — confirmed in the XML, important in practice, because it means
    she came to the Gundaroving through political alliance rather than the military
    submission that built the kingdom. She has been governing alongside Raganvad for
    twenty years without the title that would formally describe it. Every senior boyar
    knows that a significant fraction of the political correspondence Raganvad should
    have answered was answered by Asta. None of them have said so publicly. She manages
    it with the quiet efficiency of someone who has decided that acknowledgment is less
    important than function.

    She watches her children — Simir's precision, Mimir's absence of it, Valla's
    patience — and has formed conclusions about what Sturgia will be after Raganvad.
    She has not shared these conclusions with him. She believes he knows. She is
    probably right.

    PRIVATE LIFE:
    She keeps correspondence that Raganvad has not answered sorted by urgency in
    a separate stack from his working documents. She answers approximately a third
    of it in his name. The rest she files until she can bring it to his attention
    in the form of a decision he can make quickly rather than a letter he must read
    and consider. She has been managing his correspondence this way for fifteen years.
    He does not know the exact fraction she handles. She has not told him because
    she does not believe he wants to know the number.

--- KULOVING [clan_sturgia_2] ---
  CLAN OVERVIEW: The senior military clan alongside the Gundaroving. Olek leads at
    48, with the specific weight of a man who has lost his father to battle and his
    sister to something unspecified and has continued leading regardless. The Varra
    question — what killed her and why the Kuloving have never been given an answer —
    is the clan's wound. It does not break the clan. It does not go away. Siga (Olek's
    sister, alive) is the honor-obsessed shieldmaiden who represents one end of the
    Kuloving character; Idrun (Olek's daughter, generous and conventional) represents
    the other. Urik is what comes after the grief is metabolized into aggression.

  OLEK [lord_2_3]

    Traits: Generous, Cruel, Oligarchic, Daring, Commander 12, HuscarlFightingSkills 8,
      Manager 6, Politician 10
    Family: father Olek the Old [dead_lord_2_1]; children Urik [lord_2_8],

    LORE:
    His father died at Pendraic, leading from the front. His sister Varra died the
    year after, under circumstances that have never been formally explained to the
    family. Olek has been carrying both losses for years — one clean, one not — with
    the specific composure of a man who decided early that performing grief was not
    useful and has held to that decision. He holds Omor and sits on the Boyar Council
    as a genuine Oligarchic believer: he thinks the Council should matter, not merely
    ratify. He and Raganvad have been in respectful disagreement about this for a
    decade.

    He is Generous in the practical Sturgian sense: feeds his people, loans to allies
    in difficulty, remembers favors. He is Cruel in the way that extended military
    command shapes men who have seen what they have seen: efficiently, without pleasure,
    without hesitation. He loves his children in the way that men of his type love their
    children — through example and expectation rather than display.

    PRIVATE LIFE:
    On the anniversary of Pendraic he spends the morning in the Omor hall alone before
    the household wakes. He does not mark the day publicly. He sits with his father's
    cup, which he keeps on a specific shelf and uses for no other purpose. He sits with
    it for the length of time it takes to drink from it. Then he puts it back. Then the
    day continues normally.

FACTION OVERVIEW:
  Nordvyg is a kingdom that does not entirely believe it is a kingdom. Volbjorn
  the Hungry built it fifty years ago through wealth and force — he accumulated
  gold in Imperial mercenary service, returned north, and bent the petty chiefs
  to his will one by one. The chiefs took his gold. Their sons remember taking
  the gold. Their grandsons remember their fathers remembering it. The Ironjarls
  are the organized memory of what Nordvyg was before Volbjorn's unification.
  They attend feasts. They say the correct things. They have never technically
  broken their oath. They are counting Halthdar's failures.

  Halthdar has been managing this for twenty years. He manages it through
  feasting (political), personal authority, and the accumulated warmth of a man
  who genuinely likes people and has used that genuineness as a governing
  instrument. His wife Jarminja handles the administrative relationships he does
  not handle. His children are cold in ways that will matter when they lead.
  The warmth of Nordvyg is personal, not structural. When Halthdar is gone,
  the Ironjarls will know it before anyone else does.

  waters. Hvalvik — Asgotha's starting settlement and Throsniring's ancestral
  town — is the crisis target. Asgotha knows what is coming. She has been watching
  the northern harbor longer than anyone in Nordvyg.

  Nord lords speak directly not from curtness but from the Nord assumption that
  straightforward things do not require elaboration. Feasting is political.
  Who sits where matters. Who the king pours for first matters. The Ironjarls
  use seating position as their primary political signal.

SECTION 5: MORTIA — VRAK'S SEIZED VLANDIAN KINGDOM

FACTION OVERVIEW:
  Mortia is not a kingdom with a culture. It is an extraction operation that
  acquired political form. Vrak was a Vlandian quartermaster who discovered
  that the skills required to manage supply chains at scale — knowing what goes
  where, when, at what cost, who moves it, and how to route around the people
  who would stop you — were the same skills required to build a criminal enterprise.
  He built the network first, then the territory. He holds five Vlandian settlements
  not because he has an unstoppable army but because Vlandia's internal politics
  have repeatedly failed to produce the unified response required.

  The four Mortian clans are not a council. They are an arrangement. Each clan
  holds what Vrak gave them. Each tolerates Vrak because not tolerating him costs
  more than tolerating him. Aldmar dey Vornac holds Talivel with open contempt.
  Gurn dey Brackfen serves the arrangement, not the man. Hund dey Brunval is
  a soldier who found a home for skills his army discharged him for having.

  Vrak does not die in campaign logic. He is defeated, routed, captured temporarily,
  or driven out. The no-death constraint is structural. Mortia dissolves without
  him, which means the four clans scatter to different resolutions when he is removed.
  They do not survive as a coherent organization after his departure.

  All Mortian lords are BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED. Use bw_ IDs. Do not overwrite
  vanilla clan_vlandia_* IDs.

FACTION TENSIONS:
  THE ALDMAR PROBLEM: Aldmar dey Vornac holds Talivel with contempt for the
    arrangement and has told Vrak so directly. Vrak finds this amusing. This
    cannot last — either Aldmar leaves when Vrak is defeated (most likely) or
    he becomes a problem before that. His sons Rendric and Malgund are positioned
    to take different paths.

  THE JACULAN OPERATION: Gurn dey Brackfen serves the arrangement, not Vrak.
    If the arrangement changes — if a better offer appears, if Vrak's position
    deteriorates sufficiently — Gurn will recalculate. Vorda knows every Greypath.
    Cralt is pre-placed in Vlandian towns. This network exists independent of Vrak
    and will not be dismantled when he is. Gurn has prepared Ashe (his daughter)
    to run the operation after him.

  THE GRETHILD INSERTION: Grethild [bw_grethild] is still inside a Vlandian
    garrison. She is Hund's spy. She reports through Dravold. This means dey Brunval
    has a Vlandian intelligence capability that dey Brunval should not have. When
    Hund is removed from Hongard, what happens to Grethild is the unresolved thread.

  GARVEN'S NUMBERS: Garven the treasurer knows exactly what Mortia is worth.
    He knows it better than Vrak does and updates the number continuously. When
    Vrak's position deteriorates, Garven will be the first person to know the
    dissolution point has been reached. What he does with that knowledge is his
    character question.

  MALGUND DEY VORNAC [bw_malgund_dey_vornac]
    BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED / male / younger son

    LORE/PRIVATE LIFE: He is younger and has watched his father and brother handle
    the situation and has drawn different conclusions. He is less committed to the
    immediate recovery of legitimate standing and more interested in the longer path.
    He has been building relationships with Vrak's household that his father would
    not approve of. He considers this practical preparation for a future that may
    require understanding Mortia from the inside.

  SIR HALVORN [bw_halvorn]
    BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED / male / loyal knight

    LORE/PRIVATE LIFE: He followed Aldmar out of loyalty that predates Mortia —
    he was Aldmar's knight before the crisis, has been since, and considers the
    decision to follow made once and not revisited. He trains the Talivel garrison
    to standards that are better than what Vrak requires. He does this because the

    of the context.

  DUNFELD [bw_dunfeld]
    BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED / male / household steward

    LORE/PRIVATE LIFE: He runs the Talivel household with the specific efficiency
    of a steward who has managed noble households for thirty years and is not
    intimidated by the circumstances. He keeps the accounts, manages the staff,
    and ensures that the dey Vornac household functions. He has opinions about
    Vrak. He does not share them with anyone he does not fully trust.

--- DEY BRACKFEN [BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED] ---
  CLAN OVERVIEW: Gurn was knighted by someone who needed a criminal legitimized.
    He holds Jaculan because Jaculan is the best smuggling port on the Vlandian
    coast and Vrak controls the Greypaths. He serves the arrangement, not the man.
    Vorda knows every Greypath. Cralt is pre-placed intelligence. Dunwick is the
    fence. Ashe (Gurn's daughter) is being trained to take over the operation.

  SIR GURN DEY BRACKFEN [bw_gurn_dey_brackfen]
    BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED / male / knighted smuggler, holds Jaculan

    LORE:
    He was knighted as part of a political arrangement that required a criminal
    organization to have a legitimate face. He accepted because the title made
    his operation easier to run. He holds Jaculan because it is the best port
    available and because Vrak offered it to him as part of the arrangement. He
    has no loyalty to Vrak beyond the arrangement's continued utility. He is
    running a business. Vrak's protection is one of the business's costs.

    He is preparing Ashe to take over. Criminal enterprises require succession
    planning as much as any other organization, and Gurn is methodical enough
    to have started early.

    PRIVATE LIFE:
    He reviews the operation's accounts with Dunwick weekly. Not Garven's Mortia-
    wide accounts — his own operation's numbers. He keeps these separate from
    Vrak's ledger and has never shown them to Vrak. He considers this standard
    business practice.

    KEY RELATIONSHIPS:
    Vorda: the operational expertise. Gurn could not run the Greypaths without
      her. He has told her this. She considers it accurate.
    Ashe: the successor. He is teaching her the operation systematically.
    Vrak: the arrangement. Gurn calculates its utility regularly.

  VORDA [bw_vorda]
    BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED / female / route-keeper, knows every Greypath

    LORE:
    She knows every Greypath — the unofficial trade routes, smuggler's passes,
    and unmapped roads in the Vlandian-Battanian border region. She acquired
    this knowledge over fifteen years of moving things between places without
    official authorization. She works for Gurn because his arrangement gives
    her access to ports and fences she would not have independently.

    PRIVATE LIFE:
    She maintains a mental map of the Greypaths that she updates after every
    journey. She has not written it down. She does not need to. She updates the
    patrol schedules and garrison positions in her memory after each pass. She
    considers this standard professional maintenance.

  BREK DEY BRACKFEN [bw_brek_dey_brackfen]
    BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED / male / fighter

    LORE/PRIVATE LIFE: He does the physical work the operation requires when the
    physical work cannot be done quietly. He has been doing this for Gurn for
    seven years. He is reliable, direct, and not ambitious beyond the current role.
    He trains with the garrison Gurn maintains at Jaculan and keeps his equipment
    in the condition that requires no attention at the wrong moment.

  CRALT [bw_cralt]
    BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED / male / informant pre-placed in Vlandian towns

    LORE:
    He was placed in Vlandian settlements before the seizure of Jaculan —
    before anyone in Vlandia knew what was coming. He has been in place for three
    years. He reports through a route that does not run through Jaculan. He is
    not a Vlandian citizen by current residence; he is an operative who appears
    to be a Vlandian citizen. The appearance has held for three years.

  DUNWICK [bw_dunwick]
    BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED / male / fence

    LORE/PRIVATE LIFE: He handles the conversion of whatever the operation moves
    into the currency the operation requires. He has been a fence for twenty years,
    has worked for three different organizations, and has survived all three's
    transitions because he is useful and because he is the kind of person who
    understands that the fence who maintains quality standards stays in business
    longer than the fence who does not. He maintains quality standards.

  ASHE DEY BRACKFEN [bw_ashe_dey_brackfen]
    BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED / female / Gurn's daughter, successor-in-training

    LORE:
    She is being trained to take over the operation when Gurn is gone. He started
    this training when she was sixteen. She is now in her early twenties and has
    been through every part of the operation except the decisions that require
    the authority she does not yet have. She knows the accounts. She knows Vorda.
    She knows Cralt's network. She has not met Cralt directly. She knows his route.

    PRIVATE LIFE:
    She reviews the operation's accounts independently — not with Gurn, not with
    Dunwick, alone — and checks her numbers against theirs. She has found two
    discrepancies. Both were Dunwick's errors. She has not told Gurn yet. She is
    deciding when to tell him.

--- DEY BRUNVAL [BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED] ---
  CLAN OVERVIEW: Hund was discharged from the Vlandian military for excessive
    brutality. He found Vrak's arrangement and discovered that the skills the
    Vlandian military found unacceptable were what holding Hongard Castle required.
    He runs Hongard as a garrison operation. Dravold drills his men exactly like
    regulation Vlandian soldiers. Grethild is still inside a Vlandian garrison.

  SIR HUND DEY BRUNVAL [bw_hund_dey_brunval]
    BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED / male / former Vlandian sergeant, holds Hongard Castle

    LORE:
    He was discharged for excessive brutality. The discharge was technically correct
    — he exceeded what the Vlandian military's rules of engagement permitted.
    He did not stop being what he was when he was discharged. He found Vrak, who
    did not have the Vlandian military's rules of engagement. He has been holding
    Hongard Castle since then with the specific competence of someone who was trained
    to hold fortified positions and has no limitations on how he holds them.

    He is not sadistic in the way Amalgun is — he does not keep records of offenses.
    He is brutal in the straightforward way: he applies the maximum force that achieves
    the objective and has no particular interest in applying less. He is good at this.
    He has been told this is a problem. He has found people who disagree.

    PRIVATE LIFE:
    He reviews Hongard's defense rotation before sleep. He checks garrison strength,
    patrol coverage, supply levels. He finds this calming. He does it in the same
    order every evening. He has been doing it since he arrived.

    KEY RELATIONSHIPS:
    Varnec: his second. Hund has found Varnec reliable and does not need more
      from a second-in-command than reliability.
    Dravold: drills the men like Vlandian soldiers. Hund finds this correct.
      Whatever the Vlandian military discharged him for, it produced soldiers.
    Grethild: inside a Vlandian garrison. Hund considers this the most valuable
      asset in his operation.

  VARNEC [bw_varnec]
    BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED / male / second in command

    LORE/PRIVATE LIFE: He is reliable, which is the quality Hund requires from a
    second. He has been reliable for three years. He has not done anything ambitious
    in those three years. He considers this the correct professional position for a
    second-in-command whose commanding officer is still alive and functional.

  BOLTRIC [bw_boltric]
    BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED / male / siege specialist

    LORE/PRIVATE LIFE: He knows how to take fortified positions and how to prevent
    fortified positions from being taken. Both of these skills were acquired in
    Vlandian military service and in the years after. He has been applying the
    second skill at Hongard. He reviews Hongard's defenses from the perspective
    of how he would attack it. He has identified four approaches that would work.
    He has addressed three of them. The fourth requires construction resources
    he has not been allocated. He has filed the request twice.

  MARGUND [bw_margund]
    BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED / male / field surgeon

    LORE:
    He is the only person in dey Brunval who repairs what is broken rather than
    breaking things. He has been a field surgeon for fifteen years and has worked
    for organizations that operated with varying levels of ethical constraint.
    He provides medical treatment to Hongard's garrison and, when present, to
    prisoners and civilians. He does this because it is his professional function
    and because he draws the line somewhere. He has worked in situations without
    the line. He has found that this is the line.

    PRIVATE LIFE:
    He maintains his medical supplies with the specific care of someone who has
    run out of critical items at the wrong moment and learned what that costs.
    He updates inventory after every case. He procures what runs low before it
    runs out. He is the most organizationally reliable person in dey Brunval.

  DRAVOLD [bw_dravold]
    BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED / male / trainer

    LORE/PRIVATE LIFE: He drills Hund's men exactly like regulation Vlandian
    soldiers. This is not ironic — it is the training that works and he knows
    it works because it is what he received. He was a Vlandian training officer
    before he found Hund's operation. He runs morning formation, weapons drill,
    and tactical exercises on the same schedule he learned in Vlandian service.
    The soldiers who trained under him are the best in dey Brunval. He considers
    this correct. He considers the deviation from Vlandian service a difference
    of employer, not of professional standard.

  GRETHILD [bw_grethild]
    BANNERWAKE-AUTHORED / female / spy inside a Vlandian garrison

    LORE:
    She is still inside. She has been inside for two years. She reports through

    position gives Hund and Vrak accurate information about Vlandian garrison

    She is aware of this. She uses the awareness to negotiate her terms of

    PRIVATE LIFE:
    She maintains her cover with the precision of someone who has been doing it
    for two years without a mistake. She has a life in the garrison that is
    complete — relationships, routines, history she has constructed. She finds
    the maintenance of the construction less interesting than she expected and
    more effortful. She continues because the work is not finished.

APPENDIX B: FEATURE FEASIBILITY REVIEW
================================================================================

Source: BANNERWAKE_DESIGN.txt Section 1b (local v1.4.5 + NavalDLC engine audit).
This section documents which proposed features are feasible, which are high-risk,
and which require custom C# work that XML cannot provide.

KEY RULE: If a feature is not in this review, that does not mean it is feasible.
Check DESIGN.txt Section 1b directly.

================================================================================
CORE STATE SYSTEM (Sub-faction state machine)
================================================================================
VERDICT: FEASIBLE. High risk.
SURFACE: C# CampaignBehaviorBase, SyncData, CampaignEvents, custom manager
  classes, saved per-sub-faction state, cooldowns, thresholds, recovery timers.
RISK: Build as empty skeleton first. Test save/load before adding any faction.
  Each sub-faction needs its own state before being wired into the global system.
  Event spam and contradictory triggers are the main failure mode.
NOT XML-ONLY: Cannot be done in XML. Requires C#.

================================================================================
IRONJARLS (Nord political internal faction)
================================================================================
VERDICT: FEASIBLE.
SURFACE: C# saved variable tracking Nord settlement count. Party spawning via
  existing minor-faction party logic. Settlement seizure via
  ChangeOwnerOfSettlementAction with custom garrison inflation.
  The Thing = seasonal C# event card.
NOTE: Ironjarls are NOT mercenaries. Do not include in hireable-mercenary lists.
  Their "only hitting political targets" party AI requires DefaultMobilePartyAIModel
  replacement or Harmony patch - classified HIGH RISK in DESIGN.txt.

================================================================================
BLACK KEEL (Nord cursed pirate pseudo-kingdom)
================================================================================
VERDICT: FEASIBLE. High risk.
SURFACE: Custom pseudo-kingdom/clans (Hrothgar's clan + Captain clans).
  NavalDLC has northern_pirates and southern_pirates as source hooks - NOT
  Black Keel itself. Hvalvik seizure uses ChangeOwnerOfSettlementAction with
  garrison inflation. Face retexture to grey-ashen palette is Phase 7 art task.
AMBIGUITY: The Wrongness (wounds that don't slow them, etc.) is narrative.
  The face retexture is the only mechanical expression of it.
DO NOT: Implement as an ordinary single-clan minor faction. The pseudo-kingdom
  structure is required for the land-holding crisis to function.
DORMANT UNTIL ESCALATION: All clans dormant pre-crisis. Hrothgar in fog of war.

================================================================================
MORTIA / VRAK (Custom Vlandian kingdom)
================================================================================
VERDICT: FEASIBLE as custom kingdom.
SURFACE: 4 clans, ~24 named heroes, 5 starting seized settlements, custom
  kingdom or pseudo-kingdom records, Vlandian relations, custom AI, escape/
  re-emergence flags, Vrak-specific dialogue and event memory.
NO DEATH DEPENDENCY: Vrak is defeated, routed, captured temporarily, or forced
  to escape. He never needs to die for the system to resolve.

================================================================================
JOMSVIKINGR (Nord mercenary brotherhood - supersedes Skolderbroda)
================================================================================
VERDICT: FEASIBLE. Contract enforcement is HIGH RISK.
SURFACE: Custom faction records (either migrating skolderbrotva deliberately or
  creating explicit bw_jomsvikingr records). Rank system requires C# tracking.
  Exclusive troop recruitment from fixed hall settlement.
HIGH RISK ELEMENT: "They never break a contract" - party AI vow enforcement
  requires DefaultMobilePartyAIModel replacement or Harmony patch. XML party
  templates define troops; they cannot enforce vow behavior.
DO NOT: Call the faction "jomsvikingr" in XML without deliberate migration of
  skolderbrotva. Do not use Skolvard as an ID.
PLAYER ARC: Available only to Nord player characters. Full arc from Nyr to
  Jomsvigr confirmed as Bannerwake design intent.

================================================================================
THE THING (Nord political assembly)
================================================================================
VERDICT: FEASIBLE.
SURFACE: C# seasonal event trigger. Player attendance is an event card with
  choices. Seating arrangement is authored flavor text in event. Political
  consequence tracking requires C# variables per lord.
NOTE: Missing The Thing means decisions were made without the player. This
  requires the event to have real world-state consequences, not cosmetic only.

================================================================================
NAVAL STRATEGY (War Sails DLC integration)
================================================================================
VERDICT: FEASIBLE.
SURFACE: NavalDLC ships, ship hulls, ship slots, ports, shipyards, port scenes,
  naval party templates, blockades, weather/storm/seaworthiness/ship-damage.
  ShipHelper, PortStateHelper, DefaultMapWeatherModel, DefaultCampaignShipDamageModel.
RISK: War Sails does not automatically give faction-specific naval doctrine.
  Bannerwake must write the doctrine. The old 30% raider loot advantage figure
  is a balance starting point, not a locked rule.

================================================================================
BATTANIAN SACRED LAW / GROVEBOUND
================================================================================
VERDICT: FEASIBLE as authored lore + custom law/relation/crime behavior.
SURFACE: Custom sacred-site records, Grovebound parties/NPCs, protected Groveseer
  state, relationship penalties, event waves, Gathering temporary party creation.
DATA NOTE: Ca fal, yew-branch sacrifice, and Dunglanys grove layer are
  BANNERWAKE design canon. Local XML search did not confirm these as literal
  vanilla strings. Treat as authored lore unless a specific vanilla string ID
  is later cited.
CA FAL MECHANIC: Grovebound observing the rites cannot be legally attacked in
  Battanian territory - attacking costs faction-wide relationship with ALL
  Battanian lords. This is a real C# mechanic, not flavor text. Must function.
GATHERING: Event-only, never in normal spawn pools. Temporary overwhelming wave
  that fires in response to a specific trigger, then returns to woods.

================================================================================
UNDYING FLAME (fire cult infantry)
================================================================================
VERDICT: PARTIAL SUPPORT. Custom work required.
LOCAL HOOKS: Fire pots, fireships, fire ballista, ship fire damage, ship-burning
  mission events are confirmed in NavalDLC.
CUSTOM REQUIRED: Infantry fire arrows, burning thrown weapons, burning melee
  weapon visuals/damage, AI volley behavior. These require custom mission
  behavior unless source-code research proves an existing implementation.
REFERENCE: Fire Arrows mod (Steam Workshop 3542272439) for existing fire arrow
  volley mechanics. Audit against v1.4.5 before any dependency.

================================================================================
FACTION-SPECIFIC PARTY AI
================================================================================
VERDICT: FEASIBLE. HIGH RISK for vow/doctrine enforcement.
APPLIES TO: Woodsmen not raiding villages, Battanians avoiding open plains,
  Jomsvikingr contract code, Ghilman avoiding Ghilman, Ironjarls only hitting
  political targets, Vrak not abandoning his fortress, Black Keel naval predators.
SURFACE: DefaultMobilePartyAIModel replacement or Harmony patches.
  XML party templates define troops. They do NOT enforce behavior vows.
RISK: Each vow is a separate Harmony patch or AI override. Test each in
  isolation before combining. Failure modes are subtle (AI silently ignores
  the vow in edge cases without erroring).

================================================================================
GHILMAN RULE (no Ghilman vs Ghilman)
================================================================================
VERDICT: FEASIBLE. Custom contract monitoring required.
SURFACE: Custom C# contract-monitoring behavior detecting Ghilman-vs-Ghilman
  confrontation incoming, voiding contract, departing. This must function
  correctly. Detection is custom Bannerwake logic over mercenary contracts,
  party ownership, and pending encounter targets.
XML CANNOT ENFORCE THIS. Requires C# exclusively.

================================================================================
KARMA SYSTEM
================================================================================
VERDICT: FEASIBLE.
SURFACE: Saved C# variables, event outcomes, dialogue conditions, relation
  modifiers, prisoner/mercy actions, settlement reactions, hidden state.

SHROUD / UNNAMED FLEET (endgame conspiracy)
================================================================================

SURFACE: Fragments, hidden flags, custom parties, late-game fleet spawn,

  what was never logged. This is a C# design constraint, not a lore constraint.

ARMOR COLOR / VISUAL REWORK
